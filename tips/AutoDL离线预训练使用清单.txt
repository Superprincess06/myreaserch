===================================================================
AutoDL 离线预训练使用清单（全基线）
===================================================================

已准备好的本地预训练权重目录：
weights/pretrained/

文件清单：
- weights/pretrained/vgg16.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/resnet50.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/densenet121.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/mobilenetv3_small_100.lamb_in1k.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/mobilenetv3_large_100.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/shufflenet_v2_x1_0.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/ghostnet_100.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/convnextv2_atto.safetensors
- weights/pretrained/efficientnet_b0.safetensors

在 AutoDL 上单模型离线训练命令（项目根目录执行）：

PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/vgg16.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh vgg16
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/resnet50.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh resnet50
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/densenet121.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh densenet121
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/mobilenetv3_small_100.lamb_in1k.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh mobilenetv3_small
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/mobilenetv3_large_100.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh mobilenetv3_large
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/shufflenet_v2_x1_0.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh shufflenetv2
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/ghostnet_100.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh ghostnet
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/convnextv2_atto.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh convnextv2
PRETRAINED_PATH=weights/pretrained/efficientnet_b0.safetensors bash scripts/run_single.sh efficientnet

验证是否离线成功：
- 日志中不应出现 huggingface/pytorch 远程下载进度条。
- 会出现本地预训练加载统计信息：loaded / skipped / missing / unexpected。
- 随后正常进入 epoch 训练。
